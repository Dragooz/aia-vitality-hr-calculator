Before you trust the number

  • AIA counts your average heart rate across the session, not your peak. A hard sprint inside an easy walk will not carry the average.
  • The session must be one unbroken workout. Two 15-minute walks are not a 30-minute session.
  • Heart rate points cap at 15,000 per membership year. Step points cap separately at 10,000.
  • Your watch decides what it reports to AIA. If its numbers differ from this page, your watch is what gets you the points.
