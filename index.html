How AIA counts these points

Heart rate

AIA gives points for one workout each day. Two values set the points: your average heart rate and the duration of the workout.

Your maximum heart rate is 220 minus your age.

  • Targets round up. At age 27, 70% is 135.1 bpm. You must get 136 bpm.
  • AIA uses the average, not the peak. A short sprint does not lift a slow workout.
  • Do not stop the workout. Two 15-minute walks are two workouts, not one 30-minute workout.
  • AIA counts only your best workout each day.

Steps

5,000 steps: 25 points. 7,500 steps: 50 points. 12,500 steps: 100 points.

Points do not add

The daily maximum is 150 points. AIA gives the higher of your workout points and your step points. It does not add them.

Yearly limits

Heart rate: 15,000 points each membership year. Steps: 10,000 points each membership year.

If your watch shows different zones

Your watch can calculate a different maximum heart rate. AIA uses 220 minus your age. Use the targets on this page for points.
